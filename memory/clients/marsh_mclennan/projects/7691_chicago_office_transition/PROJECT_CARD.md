## InterWork Scope Summary

InterWork provides speedpacks/packing materials, move management, and transportation
only. **Local IT handles all physical disconnect and packing** — not InterWork scope.

1. **Speedpacks:** 20 delivered to 500 W Monroe (target 9/8/2026); 20 delivered to 111 N
   Canal (target 9/9/2026). Bubble wrap/cardboard separation and required equipment
   protection provided. 40 speedpacks total across both sites.
2. **500 W Monroe IT relocation → Orland Park** (target transport 9/11/2026):
   44 monitors (30 ~37", 14 ~22"), 37 docking stations, 37 keyboard/mouse sets.
3. **111 N Canal legacy inventory → Orland Park** (leaves Canal permanently, client
   prefers same day as packing if feasible): 52 monitors ~22", 0 docking stations,
   77 keyboard/mouse sets.
4. **111 N Canal reuse/return inventory → temporary storage** (returns to Canal after
   5th Floor construction, does NOT go to Orland Park): 171 monitors ~24", 110 docking
   stations, 0 keyboard/mouse sets.
5. **500 W Monroe AV:** most AV is leased, stays with the space or is handled separately
   under the lease unless directed otherwise. Roku devices (part of the leased AV
   install) stay in place unless directed otherwise. Two MMA-owned Apple TVs (lunchroom,
   boardroom) — current expectation is remove and send to Orland Park, final disposition
   still needs confirmation.
6. **500 W Monroe furniture:** existing leased furniture expected to remain — do not
   remove unless specifically identified and approved. Provisional additional items that
   may move to Orland Park (all quantities/dimensions TBD, pending additional scope):
   chairs, one table, two refrigerators, one Christmas tree.
7. **500 W Monroe decommission:** expected to be limited since most furniture/AV stays.
   Move management, final walkthrough, remove approved MMA-owned items and directed
   abandoned/personal contents, coordinate asset-management activity, support landlord
   turnover/signoff. Built-in/low-voltage wiring currently expected to remain; any
   additional low-voltage removal is pending landlord confirmation.
8. **111 N Canal hoteling:** FDOB 9/14/2026, 1st Floor. Desktop/monitor/docking/keyboard-
   mouse/printer setup and network/WAP/technology configuration are handled by
   Marsh/local IT, not InterWork, unless separately assigned.
