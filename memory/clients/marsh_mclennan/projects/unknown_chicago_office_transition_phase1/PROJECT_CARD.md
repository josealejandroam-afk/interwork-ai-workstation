## InterWork Scope Summary

InterWork provides speedpacks/packing materials, move management, and transportation
only. **Local IT handles all physical disconnect and packing** — not InterWork scope.

1. **Speedpacks:** 20 delivered to 500 W Monroe on 9/8/2026; 20 delivered to 111 N
   Canal on 9/9/2026. Bubble wrap/cardboard separation provided. ~30-35 unmounted
   24" monitors may fit per speedpack, but protection takes priority over capacity.
   More speedpacks available quickly if needed.
2. **500 W Monroe IT relocation → Orland Park** (target transport 9/11/2026):
   44 monitors (30 ~37", 14 ~22"), 37 docking stations, 37 keyboard/mouse combos.
3. **111 N Canal legacy inventory → Orland Park** (leaves Canal permanently, target
   same day as packing if feasible): 52 monitors ~22", 0 docking stations, 77
   keyboard/mouse combos.
4. **111 N Canal reuse inventory → temporary storage** (returns to Canal after 5th
   Floor construction, does NOT go to Orland Park): 171 monitors ~24", 110 docking
   stations, 0 keyboard/mouse combos.
5. **500 W Monroe AV:** most AV is leased, stays with the space unless the AV lease
   team directs otherwise — do not dispose/relocate leased AV hardware without
   direction. Roku devices (original leased AV) stay in the IT closet. Two MMA-owned
   Apple TVs (lunchroom, boardroom) go to Orland Park, subject to final IT
   confirmation.
6. **500 W Monroe furniture:** existing leased furniture stays per Article 11/Exhibit
   D — do not remove unless specifically directed. Provisional additional items that
   may move to Orland Park (quantities/dimensions all TBD): chairs, one table, two
   refrigerators, one Christmas tree.
7. **500 W Monroe decommission/cleanout:** move-management coordination, final
   walkthrough, remove approved MMA-owned items and directed abandoned contents,
   support landlord turnover/signoff. Expected to be relatively small in scope since
   most furniture/AV stays with the premises.
8. **Personal contents:** no InterWork-managed employee content move — employees
   handle their own belongings. InterWork only addresses abandoned contents per
   project-team direction during final cleanout.
9. **111 N Canal hoteling:** first day of business 9/14/2026, 1st Floor. Technology
   setup (monitors, docking, keyboard/mouse, desktop, printers, temp IT support) is
   handled by Marsh/MMA technology resources, not InterWork, unless separately
   assigned.
