## Confirmed InterWork Scope

InterWork will support phased carpet replacement by shifting furniture out of designated flooring zones and restoring the furniture after flooring work is completed. Current scope, per zone/phase:

1. Review each scheduled flooring zone based on the project phasing plan.
2. Tag and identify affected pedestals/file cabinets and task chairs so each item can be returned to its assigned workstation.
3. Unfasten pedestals/file cabinets secured underneath workstation surfaces (existing pedestals appear to be attached using brackets with Phillips-head screws).
4. Move pedestals/file cabinets outside of the active flooring area (employee contents may remain inside where they can be safely moved without accessing the contents).
5. Shift task chairs and other loose furniture as required to provide flooring access.
6. Stage relocated furniture outside of the active work zone.
7. Maintain organization and identification of furniture throughout each phase.
8. Return after flooring installation to restore completed areas.
9. Return task chairs to their assigned workstations.
10. Return pedestals/file cabinets to their corresponding workstation locations.
11. Reattach pedestal/file cabinet brackets as required.
12. Return other furniture moved by InterWork to its original location.
13. Perform minor furniture adjustments necessary to return completed areas to normal occupancy.
14. Repeat the process for each approved flooring phase.
