## Confirmed Scope

- Furniture remains in place per the property manager; no general furniture removal or decommission.
- Coworking IT move: 1-2 movers relocate monitors, keyboards, docking stations, mice, and designated boxes for 3 employees to Common Desk (3040 Post Oak Blvd). Sydney coordinates placement with the crew on-site; movers rejoin the main decommission crew after.
- General cleanout: remove/dispose everything in cabinets, drawers, storage closets, office storage, kitchen/pantry, and work areas that CRC has not packed or specifically flagged to remain. Default rule: unpacked/unflagged = disposal.
- Speed packs/C-bins: building allows the crew to fill containers on-site during the day, but filled containers cannot be removed from the building until after hours.
- Storage: CRC-retained packed items (office/kitchen supply boxes, framed photos, other designated items) go into temporary storage until CRC's new office opens — currently targeted for **December 2026**. Storage vendor/method still not confirmed (see Open Loops).
- Wall patching and sign removal: remove the identified acrylic wall sign, patch the resulting holes plus other move-out-related holes. Scope is limited patching, not extensive wall repair.
- Printers — e-waste: **1 Canon imageRUNNER ADVANCE DX C3825** (corrected 2026-08-21 — earlier notes mislabeled this a "Lexmark CX825"; it is a Canon).
- Printers — ship to Compucom depot: Lexmark MX826, CX331, CX635, and associated toner, prepared/protected for shipment.
- IT equipment: remove/e-waste remaining monitors, docking stations, keyboards, mice unless otherwise designated. Any laptops found during cleanout must NOT be e-wasted — secure and ship to the Compucom depot instead.
- Security/access control: remove access-control card readers and the IT-closet camera, install blank plates after reader removal. Cabling for removed readers may be pushed back/concealed and covered rather than fully removed; workstation cabling may remain in place; no general low-voltage cable removal required (subject to change per final property-management direction).
- Appliances: disconnect and remove the plumbed water/ice machine and the Keurig machine. **Do not remove the dishwasher** unless/until final property-management authorization is received.
- Disposal/e-waste: general office and kitchen/pantry contents, unwanted supplies, designated IT equipment and the printer above are disposed of; e-waste items are handled separately from general trash.
