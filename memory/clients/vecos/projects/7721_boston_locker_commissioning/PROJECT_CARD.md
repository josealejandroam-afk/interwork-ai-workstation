## Scope — Commissioning

InterWork is expected to **commission** the Vecos locker system at 115 Federal Street on Floors 10 and 11.

**System quantities:**
- ~100 locks total
- 4 terminals total: Floor 10 = 2 terminals, Floor 11 = 2 terminals

**Scope includes:**
- Install required cabling, system hardware, software, and terminals
- Inspect proper lock system installation
- Function-test locks
- Confirm proper network patching and connectivity
- Test and troubleshoot system issues
- Coordinate with Vecos support/PM as necessary
- Replace defective or damaged manufacturer components as required
- Enter and activate license keys at each terminal
- Verify configuration, settings, locker numbering, and procedural accuracy
- Document issues, variances, or hardware failures found during commissioning
- Complete final system testing and readiness verification
- Provide job/completion reporting
