## Project Intent

Jackson & Hertogs is significantly downsizing. The existing 909 Montgomery office has a much larger footprint than the proposed 388 Market suite, with private offices, workstation clusters, conference areas, reception, file/storage/break areas and other furniture requiring evaluation.

This is not a simple move. The working sequence is:

**Furniture planning → selective reuse → relocation → installation → decommission of everything remaining at 909 Montgomery.**

Existing furniture should be reused where practical. One partner may purchase new furniture, but that remains unconfirmed.
