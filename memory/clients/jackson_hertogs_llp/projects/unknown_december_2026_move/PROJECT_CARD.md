## Project Intent

Jackson & Hertogs is significantly downsizing. The existing 909 Montgomery office has a much larger footprint than the proposed 388 Market suite, with private offices, workstation clusters, conference areas, reception, file/storage/break areas and other furniture requiring evaluation.

This is not a simple move. The working sequence is:

**Furniture planning → selective reuse → relocation → installation → decommission of everything remaining at 909 Montgomery.**

Existing furniture should be reused where practical. One partner may purchase new furniture, but that remains unconfirmed.

## Working Scope

### Phase 1 — Design / Planning

- Review the existing furniture at 909 Montgomery.
- Use the 388 Market CAD/floor plan to determine what can physically be reused.
- Complete any additional field measurements Francisco requires.
- Develop the final furniture layout for Suite 1460.
- Divide the existing furniture into move/reuse and decommission inventories.

### Phase 2 — Move

- Protect both buildings as required.
- Pack and prepare designated contents and furniture.
- Disassemble reusable furniture at 909 Montgomery as necessary.
- Transport reusable items to 388 Market.
- Reassemble and install furniture according to the approved layout.
- Complete work during the approved December move window so the office can operate Monday.

### Phase 3 — 909 Montgomery Decommission

- Remove furniture not being reused.
- Follow the sustainable-disposition hierarchy: resale, donation, then recycling/disposal.
- Remove remaining miscellaneous contents included in the agreed scope.
- Complete vacate/clean-out work.
- Patch/paint and cable-removal obligations remain subject to client and building confirmation.
- File-room furniture and records are not expected because the file room has already been eliminated.
- No server-room decommission is expected because the client's servers are cloud based.
